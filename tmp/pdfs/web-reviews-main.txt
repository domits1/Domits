🚀  Capability Summary
To give guests a better understanding of what listing / host they are contacting, we want to implement a
reviewing system within Domits. Not only should listings and hosts recieve reviews, guests are also
reviewable.

Reviews, Intelligence, Settings, Integrations, Agentic AI

Problem Statement
Traditional vacation-rental review systems primarily focus on collecting a star rating and displaying a
written comment.
This provides limited value to Hosts and guests.
Hosts need to understand:
What guests actually liked
What disappointed them
Which operational problems repeatedly occur
Which properties are improving or declining

Guests can submit reviews after eligible stays.
Reviews are linked to reservations.
Verified stays are clearly identified.
Guests can provide overall and category ratings.
Guests can submit public and private feedback.
Hosts can respond to reviews.
Review requests can be automated.
Review reminders can be automated.
Reviews can be moderated.
Guests can dispute moderation decisions where applicable.
Hosts can dispute reviews.
Duplicate/fraudulent reviews are detected.
Reviews are analyzed for sentiment.
Review topics are automatically identified.
Review insights are available at property and portfolio level.
Reputation trends are available.
